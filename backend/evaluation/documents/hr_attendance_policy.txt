Enterprise Attendance Policy

Employees are expected to follow their assigned working schedule and maintain regular attendance.

Employees should record their attendance using the organization's approved attendance system.

If an employee cannot attend work as scheduled, the employee should inform their manager according to the applicable absence reporting process.

Repeated attendance issues may be reviewed by the employee's manager and the appropriate HR team.

Employees are responsible for ensuring that their attendance records are accurate.

This policy applies to employees across the organization.
