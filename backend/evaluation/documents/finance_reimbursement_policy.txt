Enterprise Finance Reimbursement Policy

Eligible business expenses may be reimbursed when they are submitted according to the approved reimbursement process.

Employees should submit reimbursement requests with the required receipts and supporting documents.

Reimbursement requests are reviewed by the appropriate manager or finance team.

Employees may be asked to provide additional information when documentation is incomplete.

Approved reimbursements are processed according to the company's payment schedule.
